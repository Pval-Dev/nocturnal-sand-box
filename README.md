# Nocturnal Sand Box

*Working title · In development*

Nocturnal Sand Box is a team-based Roblox battle game where you can command infantry divisions, drive tanks, pilot aircraft, or crew warships. The front changes with each round, from ground battles and armored engagements to naval combat, ship boarding, and amphibious landings.

[How a match plays](#how-a-match-plays) · [Battle modes](#battle-modes) · [Screenshots](#screenshots) · [Videos](#videos)

## How a match plays

1. **Vote and join a team.** Vote on the battle mode, victory condition, and map from compatible options.
2. **Read the briefing and deploy.** See your team's objective and choose a spawn point, division loadout, or vehicle according to the mode.
3. **Fight for the objective.** Hold territory, seize all required points, score through combat, or protect or intercept a convoy. Boarding victories can also count toward the result in compatible matches.
4. **Review the result.** See the winning team and how it won before returning to the lobby.

## Battle modes

| Mode | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Ground** | Deploy infantry and cavalry divisions, switch formations, and issue movement, follow, attack, or retreat orders. |
| **Tanks** | Fight for control points, choose ammunition, and manage repairs. Hits are resolved against armor zones and impact angles. |
| **Air** | Pilot aircraft, engage opposing planes, and contest aerial objectives. Damage to aircraft parts can affect flight. |
| **Naval** | Maneuver warships, fire cannons and torpedoes, and contest objectives at sea. Ships can take regional damage, repair, and sink. |
| **High Seas** | Fight at sea and lead troops in ship-to-ship boarding encounters. |
| **Landing** | Deploy infantry in amphibious battles where ships and shore objectives shape the fight. |

The current build has **13 configured map entries**, including Ironworks District, Valley X, Twin Islands, and dedicated convoy maps. Map availability depends on the mode and victory condition. Weather and seasonal variants can change terrain presentation and visibility.

## Loadouts and progression

- Build infantry divisions from melee, light ranged, heavy ranged, and cavalry roles. Save compositions and formations for future rounds.
- Select vehicles and save presets. Preview cosmetics, effects, and sounds in the showroom and customization area.
- Earn progression and rewards through match participation, objectives, and combat. Player profiles track unlocks and progression.
- Use mode-specific HUDs on PC or mobile, including a tactical minimap for infantry and dedicated controls and instruments for tanks, aircraft, and ships.

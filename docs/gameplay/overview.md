# Gameplay

*Nocturnal Sand Box · Working title · In development*

Two teams fight across infantry, armored, air, and naval fronts. Every round starts with votes on compatible modes, victory conditions, and maps. Players join a team, read its briefing, choose where to deploy, and take command of divisions or a vehicle. Results explain who won and how before players return to the lobby.

| Mode | Systems |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | Melee, light ranged, heavy ranged, and cavalry divisions. Configurable compositions, formations, and orders to move, follow, attack, or retreat. |
| Tanks | Armored engagements with ammunition selection, directional armor, penetration, and repairs. |
| Air | Aircraft with distinct flight profiles, aerial objectives, and damage that can affect flight. |
| Naval | Warship navigation, cannons, torpedoes, regional damage, repairs, and sinking. |
| High Seas | Naval battles with ship-to-ship boarding and infantry on deck. |
| Landing | Ships and infantry in amphibious operations with shore deployment. |

Victory can come from holding objectives, capturing all required points, scoring through combat, winning boarding encounters, or escorting or intercepting a convoy. Availability depends on the mode and map.

The build has 13 configured map entries, including Ironworks District, Valley X, Twin Islands, and dedicated convoy routes. Map selection depends on compatibility. Weather and seasonal variants can change visibility and terrain presentation.

Profiles track saved loadouts, vehicle presets, cosmetics, combat statistics, and separate mastery for infantry, tanks, air, and naval play. Match rewards and victory scores serve different purposes.
